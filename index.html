What does COP mean?

Countries meet.
Decisions matter.

COP means “Conference of the Parties.” It is the decision-making body of an international convention. The best-known COPs are held each year under the UN climate convention, but the biodiversity and desertification conventions hold their own COP meetings too.

The number tells you which meeting it is in that convention’s sequence. COP17 is the 17th meeting of the parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity, not a climate COP. Armenia was selected to host it.

COP17 in Armenia

Biodiversity is

For the first time in its history, independent Armenia will host this major international conference. More than 15,000 delegates from around 196 countries are expected to come together to discuss protecting biodiversity and building a sustainable future. Armenia’s mountains, forests, rivers, and Lake Sevan make the connection between healthy ecosystems and community wellbeing especially tangible.

A brief climate COP timeline

Milestones in climate
cooperation.

These milestones belong to the UN climate convention’s COP process. They are separate from biodiversity COP17 in Armenia.

COP1 · 1995 · Berlin

A process begins

The Berlin Mandate launched negotiations for stronger commitments to address emissions.

COP3 · 1997 · Kyoto

The Kyoto Protocol

The first treaty set binding emission-reduction targets for developed countries.

COP21 · 2015 · Paris

The Paris Agreement

Countries agreed to hold warming well below 2°C and pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5°C.

COP28 · 2023 · Dubai

A shift from fossil fuels

The first global stocktake called for transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems.

COP29 · 2024 · Baku

Climate finance

Negotiations focused on increasing climate finance for developing countries facing climate impacts.

One acronym, several conventions

Climate COP
Negotiations under the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change.

Biodiversity COP
International cooperation to conserve nature under the Convention on Biological Diversity.

Desertification COP
Global work to protect land and address drought under the UN Convention to Combat Desertification.
